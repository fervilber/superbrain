# IBM and Microsoft Announce a New AI Agent Platform Designed for Industry-Specific Transformation

Nov 10, 2026. ARMONK, N.Y. and REDMOND, Wash.

IBM (NYSE: IBM) and Microsoft (NASDAQ: MSFT) today announced Imagine Enterprise, an agentic platform designed to help enterprises build, run, and govern AI agents that help people execute industry-specific processes. IBM Consulting and Microsoft have combined their technologies and expertise with forward-deployed engineering support for qualifying engagements to help enterprises accelerate AI deployment.

Imagine Enterprise aims to help organizations—especially in regulated industries—combine human expertise with AI agents and connect them to unique enterprise data and daily processes. The platform is built to help organizations govern AI agents with controls, observability, and AI-compliance support designed to help secure industry-specific AI deployment.

IBM Consulting brings AI transformation expertise and a fleet of more than 6,000 AI agents and assets built on IBM Consulting Advantage, the company's AI-powered delivery platform. Microsoft provides the AI, data, cloud, and security foundation that is designed to help the agents operate securely across the enterprise, while forward-deployed engineers from both companies can help customers move from experimentation to production.

## Claves de la nota

- IBM Consulting aporta más de 6.000 agentes y activos de IA preconstruidos, la experiencia de haberlos construido y conocimiento sectorial.
- Microsoft aporta IA, nube y seguridad.
- La plataforma incluye soporte de ingeniería "forward-deployed" de ambas compañías.
- SEI's enterprise transformation illustrates the types of business needs that agentic AI can help address.

## Contexto de la jornada

El anuncio coincide con una oleada de lanzamientos empresariales de agentes autónomos el 11 de noviembre de 2026: Always-on Genie de Databricks, la disponibilidad general de Arc de ServiceNow, la plataforma "autonomous enterprise" de SAP con Joule Work, y SFR-AutoR&D de Salesforce. El patrón común es el paso del asistente reactivo al trabajador autónomo gobernado por identidad, permisos y auditoría.

## Contexto adicional (dealroom.co, 11 nov 2026)

IBM Ventures invested in Komodor, the maker of what it calls an Agentic Operations Platform for Production, announced November 10, 2026. The investment backs Komodor's shift from Kubernetes troubleshooting to deploying and governing AI agents across SRE, platform engineering, and DevOps workflows. IBM did not disclose the amount. The signal: IBM's move lands in a crowded field — PagerDuty introduced its Paige SRE agent in November. The challenge is scale: deploying a single agent is straightforward, but running many reliably across production systems is harder.
