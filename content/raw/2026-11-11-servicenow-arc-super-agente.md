# ServiceNow Arc: The Rise of the Super Agent for Enterprise AI

Por Kieron Allen, Cloud Wars, 11 nov 2026.

Much like the super app, the super agent is a goal that many organizations are trying to achieve. Now, ServiceNow has made Arc, its long-running personal enterprise AI agent, generally available, along with robust governance tools.

## What is Arc?

Arc is ServiceNow's personal, long-running AI agent, which moves beyond Q&A to carry out everyday tasks on behalf of users. These tasks are performed across ServiceNow workflows, email, Teams chats, and other commonly used productivity tools. Over time, as it builds memory, Arc develops skills and delivers better performance.

In terms of features, the most impressive include its ability to take ownership of tasks and, once given an objective, actively pursue it by taking autonomous actions rather than simply following instructions. Arc proactively surfaces work from areas like meetings and email inboxes. If a required integration doesn't exist, it can build one, provided it falls within a defined NVIDIA OpenShell boundary.

What really sets Arc apart, though, is how it's governed and secured. Given its access to enterprise data and systems, security is particularly important. ServiceNow says its approach extends beyond application-level controls to runtime enforcement and, in collaboration with NVIDIA, hardware-level enforcement too.

The ServiceNow Super Intelligence Control Tower defines the policies governing Arc's actions, while NVIDIA OpenShell enforces restrictions at runtime.

## AI Passport

In another development, ServiceNow has announced a further security measure that builds on the existing defenses developed alongside NVIDIA. AI Passport, which is expected to be integrated with NVIDIA's enforcement layers in a future release, is an official record that the Super Intelligence Control Tower is designed to issue to every AI agent, MCP server, and other AI asset it governs.

The Super Intelligence Control Tower draws on business context from the ServiceNow AI Platform, including decision logic and vendor risk, to determine which tools and actions each AI agent is authorized to use. In practice, ServiceNow issues the AI Passport, with the relevant business context and authorized usage rules, while NVIDIA's software and hardware enforcement layers are designed to read and enforce these permissions.
