# Building trust into every autonomous AI agent action

Ailis Claassen — TM Forum — 17 de noviembre de 2026

Communications service providers need to know that autonomous AI agents are secure, compliant and aligned with business intent **before they act**. This Catalyst adds **security and business governance agents** to the AI-native ODA Canvas to validate actions, record decisions and support trusted autonomy at scale.

## La brecha de evidencia

TM Forum Insights' 2026 research, _Trust and assurance: the keys to AI deployment at scale_, conducted in partnership with IBM Institute for Business Value, found that **72 % of CSPs believe their AI is trustworthy, but only 14 % can provide evidence to prove it**. Without stronger assurance, autonomous AI can remain in pilots, while an incorrect decision could expose revenue, regulatory compliance and customer trust.

## Guardian agents

The Catalyst project, **TrustFabric: Secure AI agents for telcos**, proposes a standard way to **evaluate every AI agent before it acts**. Built into the TM Forum **AI-native ODA Canvas**, the approach is intended to help CSPs scale autonomous AI while retaining security controls, business accountability and a record of each decision. **Guardian agents validate every proposed action**.

## Activos y estándares utilizados

- **GB1087 Agentic Interaction Security in Telecommunications** como línea base de seguridad para evaluar planes e interacciones de agentes.
- **AI-native ODA Canvas** y **AI-Native Blueprint** para situar los agentes guardianes dentro de una arquitectura sectorial compartida.
- **IG1274M AI Agent**, **IG1251D AN Agent Architecture**, **IG1251E Multi-Agent Architectures** e **IG1453 Agent to Agent Protocol for Telecoms**.
- **AI Governance Toolkit**.

## Objetivo

Producir **evidencia de que la IA autónoma opera según lo previsto**; proponer un modelo de confianza listo para usar entre agentes de distintos proveedores, sobre estándares de TM Forum; y lograr trato más justo, mayor protección de datos, decisiones explicables y **responsabilidad clara ante una persona responsable**.

> "Trust is the foundation of every AI-native enterprise. TrustFabric shows how CSPs can securely scale autonomous AI through continuous governance, runtime validation, and trusted agent collaboration across the TM Forum ODA ecosystem." — Asela Perera, CIO de Dialog Axiata PLC.
