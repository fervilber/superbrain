## AI Agent Store — Daily AI Agent News, 13 de noviembre de 2026

### United Airlines restringe el acceso de agentes de terceros

> United said it will restrict unauthorized personal AI agents and assistants from accessing its booking and reservation systems. The airline cited cybersecurity risks from shared credentials, unintended purchases or account changes, and aviation rules that require a human to complete certain check-in acknowledgments.

> Why it matters: This is a concrete warning for companies building consumer agents that act through websites: technical capability does not guarantee permission to automate. Access policies, identity verification, terms of service, and regulations may become the limiting factors.

> Try/watch: Agent builders should design explicit airline and marketplace integrations instead of relying on browser automation.

### Nectar — Creator Agent y exposición vía MCP

> Marketing platform Nectar launched Creator Agent, which searches more than 300 million creator profiles, analyzes content and prior brand partnerships, checks brand safety in context, ranks potential matches, and can send personalized outreach. It can also help coordinate contracts, product shipping, and campaign performance across Instagram and TikTok.

> The agent is available globally, and Nectar plans to make it accessible through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), a common way for AI assistants and business tools to exchange context and use external capabilities.

> Try/watch: Start with a narrowly defined campaign and require human approval before messages are sent; monitor false matches, disclosure compliance, and whether automated outreach damages creator relationships.

### Google Gemini 4 Hydrogen (resumen del día)

> Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a faster model designed for multi-step "agentic" work such as research, data analysis, coding, testing, and codebase navigation. Google says Hydrogen completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, supports four reasoning levels, and is rolling out globally in Search.
