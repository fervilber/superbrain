# Captura: Índice de Empresa Agéntica de Salesforce y su lectura analítica

> Análisis de Futurum (7 de agosto de 2026) sobre el Agentic Enterprise Index de Salesforce. Se conserva como base cuantitativa del informe: aporta las cifras de adopción, las preocupaciones dominantes y la comparación de cuota entre plataformas.

## Salesforce's Agentic Enterprise Index: A Paradigm Shift in AI Deployment

**The News:** Salesforce's 2026 Agentic Enterprise Index indicates that organizations have nearly tripled the number of active AI agents, significantly enhancing their capabilities and deployment speed[1]. The index highlights a bifurcation in deployment strategies, with consumer-facing sectors focusing on high-volume, task-specific agents, while more complex sectors such as manufacturing prioritize versatile, multistep agents. The result is a profound increase in trust and ROI, evidenced by a 4x growth in retail online sales attributed to AI agent deployments.

**Analyst Take:** The findings from Salesforce's Agentic Enterprise Index signal a significant moment for enterprise AI adoption, reshaping how organizations approach customer engagement and operational efficiency.

### Is Your AI Strategy Aligned with Market Leaders?

Salesforce's data shows that 68% of organizations are now at GenAI Stage 3 (Optimization) or higher—a figure validated by Futurum's AI Platforms Decision Maker survey, which finds 30.5% at Optimization, 24.3% at Standardization, and 13.3% at Transformation[4][5]. With 73% of enterprise software buyers ranking agentic AI as a top technology priority.

### The Challenge of Trust in AI Deployments

According to Futurum's AI Platforms Decision Maker Survey (1H2026, n=820), 55% of organizations identify reliability and hallucination management as their top adoption challenge, followed closely by privacy/security concerns at 53% [5]. In the agentic AI context specifically, security and data privacy vulnerabilities are the single biggest concern (24%), followed by loss of human control over critical decisions (16%) and integration complexity (16%).

### Salesforce vs. the Competition: Who's Winning the Agentic AI Race?

In the CRM market, Salesforce maintains a commanding 34.1% share ($29.1B in CY2025 revenue), far outpacing Adobe (6.7%), Microsoft Dynamics (5.4%), HubSpot (3.5%), ServiceNow CSM (2.7%), Zendesk (2.5%), and SAP (2.0%). However, in the AI Platforms market specifically, Salesforce holds just 0.6% share ($688M) compared to Microsoft's 12.4% ($13.7B), with ServiceNow ($1.5B, 1.3%) and SAP ($761M, 0.7%) also outpacing Salesforce in pure AI platform revenue[2]. Futurum's Signal Report on Agentic AI Platforms for Enterprise notes that Salesforce is positioning Agentforce as an "operating system for the Agentic Enterprise," using an API-first architecture to orchestrate autonomous workflows—but Microsoft's Copilot agent ecosystem benefits from its massive installed base, while ServiceNow's CY2022-25 AI platform CAGR of 182.5% signals aggressive momentum[2].

### Agentic Delivery Models: Agents vs. Copilots

A critical insight from Futurum's Enterprise Software Decision Maker survey (2H2026, n=833) reveals that 43% of enterprises expect generative AI to be delivered primarily via autonomous AI agents, compared to 31% preferring copilot/user-prompted assistants. The top deployment areas for agentic AI—IT Ops & Observability (52%), Cybersecurity (48%), and Sales/Marketing/Service (38%)—align directly with Salesforce's core CRM and customer engagement strengths, though competitors like ServiceNow dominate in IT operations and SAP holds the advantage in supply chain (34% of enterprises plan agentic deployment there).

### Are You Prepared for the Future of AI-Driven Engagement?

With 56% of organizations prioritizing customer support/CX as the top GenAI use case[5], the focus is shifting from mere automation to creating holistic, customer-centric experiences. Notably, 63% of enterprise software buyers are either actively switching vendors or open to switching depending on market conditions, and vendor pitches emphasizing superior AI capabilities are a trigger for 48% of potential switchers.

### What to Watch

- **Platform Wars:** Microsoft's Copilot agent ecosystem vs. Salesforce's Agentforce vs. ServiceNow's AI Agents—who will become the "operating system for digital labor"?
- **Market Share Shifts:** ServiceNow's AI platform revenue grew at a 182.5% CAGR (CY2022-25) vs. Salesforce's 92.8%[3].
- **Trust Issues:** With 55% of organizations citing reliability concerns and 24% flagging security as the top agentic AI concern, will governance differentiation become a competitive moat?[5]
- **Adoption Trends:** 43% of enterprises favor agent-first delivery over copilots[6].
- **Vendor Switching:** With 63% of buyers open to switching and AI capabilities as a key trigger, how will competitive dynamics reshape CRM market share?
