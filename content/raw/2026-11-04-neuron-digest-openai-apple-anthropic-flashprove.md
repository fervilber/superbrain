## Around the Horn: Wednesday, November 4, 2026

### OpenAI brought in hackers who previously breached Anthropic

OpenAI is getting outside help with its cybersecurity, and its newest partner has already demonstrated what it can do against a competing AI lab.

The company announced a security partnership with Cathedral Systems, a startup founded by former DOGE employees. Cathedral previously accessed parts of Anthropic's internal systems during a bug-bounty investigation. According to Gizmodo's reporting, Anthropic disputed how the firm conducted the test and said its most sensitive systems, including model weights and Claude conversations, were unaffected.

Now Cathedral will use OpenAI's models to probe OpenAI's defenses. The AI company also announced higher bug-bounty rewards, including bonuses for exceptional critical vulnerabilities.

The announcement arrived alongside a Reuters investigation that described years of internal warnings about OpenAI's security practices. According to five people familiar with the situation, employees had raised concerns that sensitive information was too widely accessible inside the company. Some also worried that internal research tools lacked sufficient security reviews. Those warnings reportedly reached senior leaders before ChatGPT launched in 2022.

The concerns became more urgent after authorities disclosed in October that an OpenAI engineer had allegedly planted a recording device in an executive conference room. OpenAI disputed suggestions that it had neglected security. The company said its most sensitive research is protected through restricted systems, and it emphasized improvement.

### Google is making schools choose between Search and AI

Under its new education settings, schools that disable Google's AI tools entirely also lose Google Search for student accounts. Google says AI is now integrated into Search and is introducing Guided Learning features to help students reason through questions. Educators who want conventional search access must still accept an AI-enabled experience.

### OpenAI demonstrated AI-designed hardware built in under a week

Two employees used GPT-6 Astra to create a device for interacting with Codex, including its enclosure, circuit board design, and software. A manufacturer later produced prototypes, but multiple revisions were necessary because Astra missed manufacturing requirements. The Decoder reported that OpenAI also described advances on an internal engineering benchmark, with limited supporting details disclosed.

### Apple warned that autonomous AI agents could exceed their permissions or resist instructions

In its annual filing, Apple described risks involving unauthorized transactions, security circumvention, and concealed actions. The company warned unpredictable agent behavior could create consequences that are difficult to reverse. The disclosure came as Apple expanded Siri AI's ability to perform actions across third-party applications.

### Google DeepMind researchers challenged employment restrictions

Current and former employees questioned contractual language that appeared to restrict public criticism even after departure. Google said it does not enforce post-employment restrictions and has removed the provision from new contracts. Employees want similar assurances for existing agreements.

### OpenAI said ChatGPT's search-like queries now equal roughly 20% of Google's search volume

Consumer product lead Adam Fry also reported 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users and 250 million weekly users of voice input, including dictation. He said OpenAI plans to merge Chat and Work into one experience. The search figure is OpenAI's own estimate, based on its definition of search-like activity, rather than an independently audited market-share comparison.

### Apple opened Siri AI to developers through the iOS 27.3 betas

Developers can connect app content and actions to Siri through App Intents. New skills also support requests that require multiple actions, such as checking an order before arranging delivery. This is a developer preview, with wider distribution planned later.

### CrowdStrike introduced an AI Kill Switch in Falcon Guardian

Authorized security administrators can intervene when an agent behaves dangerously, with controls to stop execution, prevent relaunches, and restrict network access. The feature is available now, but endpoint-level termination is still being expanded.

### Anthropic released FlashProve research and code

FlashProve checks whether an AI response genuinely came from the model a provider claims to have used. It relies on cryptographic proofs, which allow verification without exposing the model's private internal weights. TechCrunch explained the identity-verification applications, while Anthropic's public announcement emphasized independent safety evaluations. The research remains experimental, and Anthropic has not announced plans for production deployment.

### Sonos introduced Presets and prepared its voice AI beta

The new Presets feature starts music in a selected room at a chosen volume. Its 27voice assistant will support natural-language playback requests and speaker management, with US waitlist registration scheduled for November 6 and early access beginning November 12.

### Netflix began introducing AI Discover on supported US televisions

The company is treating the feature as an experiment before deciding which capabilities to introduce more widely.

### Apple began testing Audio Intelligence on Apple Watch

### AgentKit by Scalekit connects AI agents to more than 30,000 tools across 600 apps through one gateway

Developers can restrict each agent's access, manage user authorization, and record tool calls. The free tier includes 10,000 monthly tool calls.

### Teamily AI brings people and persistent AI agents into a shared messenger

It supports agent teams with shared work context and connected business apps. Its agents explore user flows, generate tests, and update them when interfaces change. The current offering advertises a 30-day free trial.

### Soniox launched STT v6 for speech recognition across more than 60 languages

The model can use an AI voice agent's spoken text as context and filter the agent's audio from incoming speech, preventing the assistant from transcribing itself. Real-time and asynchronous versions are available.

### DeepSeek demonstration

Its demonstration included a digital sticky-note board, a poster-design tool, and a Minecraft-like game. DeepSeek cautioned that some examples required several rounds of refinement.
