# Agentic Orchestration for Enterprise

Futurum Research — Signal Report — 17 de noviembre de 2026

Futurum Signal: _Agentic Orchestration for Enterprise_ evaluates 11 vendors on their ability to coordinate, govern, and audit multi-agent work in production. **Microsoft, Salesforce, and ServiceNow lead the Elite Zone** as enterprises shift from deploying agents to controlling them.

Agentic orchestration for enterprise is moving from pilots into production, as organizations shift from asking _whether_ to deploy AI agents to _how_ to coordinate and govern dozens of them across systems. Futurum Signal: _Agentic Orchestration for Enterprise_ provides a structured assessment of 11 vendors on their ability to:

- run multi-agent work in documented enterprise production,
- maintain state across long-running tasks,
- enforce controls while agents act, and
- govern agents built by other vendors.

Futurum Research projects **Operations & Workflow spending on AI platforms will grow from $15.4 billion in 2025 to $92.2 billion in 2030, a 43.1% CAGR**, the fastest-growing use case in its forecast.

## La gobernanza, no el enrutado, separa a los líderes

A core theme in the report is that **governance, not routing logic, now separates the leaders**. Excellence in the **Elite Zone**, where Microsoft, Salesforce, and ServiceNow lead, is defined by turning large enterprise footprints into **authoritative control planes** that enforce runtime guardrails, manage agent identities, and capture cross-vendor agent activity at scale.

The **Leader Zone** features AWS, Google Cloud, IBM, and UiPath, each with deep orchestration runtimes and strong standards alignment, while **Databricks and LangChain in the Established Zone** and **OpenAI and SAP in the Aspiring Zone** deliver targeted capabilities but have fewer verifiable production references and out-of-the-box governance controls.

## Conclusión del analista

Moving forward, market leadership will depend on how quickly vendors turn protocol support for **MCP and A2A into audit-ready evidence** and prove that autonomous multi-agent coordination delivers measurable business returns.
