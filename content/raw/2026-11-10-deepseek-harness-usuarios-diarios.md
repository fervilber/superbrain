# DeepSeek's AI Agent Platform Hits 1.54 Million Daily Users

Bloomberg · 8 de noviembre de 2026 · divulgado por DeepSeek en X el domingo 8 de noviembre

## Cifras centrales (verbatim)

> On Sunday, the Hangzhou-headquartered startup disclosed on X that DeepSeek Harness, its open-source agent application, had reached 1.54 million daily active users by November 4, less than three months after its August 13 debut. Daily users were at about 271,000 at the end of September, right after it released a desktop version of the app, based on the number of users connecting to its own servers.
>
> DeepSeek, the Chinese AI disruptor, released striking adoption numbers for its AI agent infrastructure as it continues to challenge Anthropic PBC and OpenAI with an entire swath of applications built around open-source AI technologies.

## Arquitectura y comunidad de plugins (The Business Times / Bloomberg, 9 nov 2026)

> The startup is building Harness as a "**everything is a plug-in**" architecture.
>
> A plug-in adds a special feature to a piece of software without changing it, giving it added functionality. **DeepSeek counts about 35,000 plug-ins and 60 per cent of users have built at least one of them**, though the startup said that the sophistication of the plug-ins varies.
>
> This reflects a broader shift in the AI industry, with **the battle for users increasingly fought over ecosystems and integration, rather than model performance.**

## Divulgación del equipo Harness (X, 8 nov 2026)

> DeepSeek Harness just reported 1.54M daily users and about 35,000 community plugins. [...] The update came from the Harness team, DeepSeek's open-source agent runtime. As of Nov 4, counting official-API users only, it has 1.54 million daily active users.
>
> Since DeepSeek Harness launched, more and more people have been working and exploring in it, and more and more developers have been extending what it can do through plugins. Today we'd like to share [...] "Growing Together with Our Plugin Community".
