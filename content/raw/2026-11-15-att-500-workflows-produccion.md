# AT&T puts nearly 500 agentic workflows into production

Fuente: AI Agent Store — Daily AI Agent News, domingo 15 de noviembre de 2026.

## What changed (verbatim)

> AT&T has deployed nearly 500 autonomous AI workflows across core operations and says its platform now handles more than 1 million transactions each month. The company processes about 45 billion AI tokens daily and plans to triple its production workflow count over the next year.

> AT&T's Business Agent Foundry is redesigning whole processes—not merely adding chatbots to existing steps. Current use cases include customer provisioning, network operations, sales prospecting, knowledge retrieval, and planning actions across legacy systems.

## Why it matters (verbatim)

> This is a useful benchmark for large operators: the value is coming from connecting agents to business rules, data, and older systems, not from deploying a large number of independent assistants. AT&T also requires security, privacy, and compliance review for customer-facing workflows, with human oversight until accuracy reaches the mid-to-high 90% range within an acceptable risk profile.

> Operators should identify one measurable workflow with high transaction volume, map every handoff and exception, and set an explicit threshold for when autonomy is allowed. The reported scale is company-reported and should not be treated as independent proof of performance.

## Try/watch (verbatim)

> Track whether AT&T can triple production volume without a corresponding rise in exception handling, security incidents, or human review costs.

## Lectura técnica

- **Cifras de escala**: ~500 flujos autónomos en producción, >1 millón de transacciones/mes, ~45.000 millones de tokens/día, plan de triplicar el número de flujos en un año.
- **El valor no está en el asistente sino en la integración**: el punto no es desplegar muchos asistentes independientes, sino conectar agentes a reglas de negocio, datos y sistemas legacy («planning actions across legacy systems»).
- **Umbral explícito de autonomía**: la compañía exige revisión de seguridad, privacidad y cumplimiento para los flujos de cara al cliente, con supervisión humana hasta que la precisión llega a la franja media-alta de los 90.
- **Cautela**: son cifras declaradas por la empresa; no hay verificación independiente.
