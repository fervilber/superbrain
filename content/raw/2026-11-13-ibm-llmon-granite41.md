# IBM — LLMON y los previews Granite 4.1 3B/8B (12 de noviembre de 2026)

## join.dev — AI news for Nov 13, 2026

> IBM previews LLMON models for explicitly structured prompts — IBM released experimental Granite 4.1 3B and 8B previews on November 12 that use LLMON to distinguish instructions, data and their relationships. The accompanying toolkit supports evaluation of this approach to prompt structure; the models are not intended for production.

## Polyemergence — Emerging Intelligence Archive, 2026-11-13

> LLMON: An LLM-Native Markup Language, now in Granite 4.1 3B and 8B — Researchers have developed LLMON, an LLM-native markup language [designed to make the structure of a prompt explicit, separating instructions, data and the relationships between them].
