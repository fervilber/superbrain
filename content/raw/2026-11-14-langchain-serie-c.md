# LangChain — Serie C de 300 M$ a valoración de 3.000 M$ (13 de noviembre de 2026)

Captura por extractos del blog oficial de LangChain, de TechCrunch, del comunicado de BusinessWire y del análisis de CapitalG. Fecha de captura: 2026-11-14.

## Blog oficial (Harrison Chase, 13 de noviembre de 2026)

> Today, we're announcing that LangChain raised $300M at a $3B valuation to advance our mission to enable every company to own its intelligence. The round was led by existing investor CapitalG, with significant participation from new investors GIC and DFJ Growth and existing investors IVP and Sapphire Ventures.

> We believe agents will transform the economy. That future will be shaped by domain-specific agents differentiated by their knowledge, data, and UX. These agents will be sold as standalone products and also used to transform internal company operations. The companies that control these systems will build lasting competitive advantages.

> That's why we've built LangSmith, the framework-agnostic platform to run, improve, and govern agents. More than 7,000 organizations already rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups like Harvey, Rippling, and Listen Labs to Fortune 500 enterprises like NVIDIA, Toyota, and Salesforce. Clay uses LangSmith to monitor hundreds of millions of agent runs per month and improve their agents. Workday uses LangSmith to run and govern agents across the organization, reducing the time to deploy a new agent from five months to under a day.

### Los tres pilares de LangSmith

> **Agent Runtime** gives teams the architecture and infrastructure to run agents reliably. Deep Agents, LangGraph, and LangChain give developers flexibility across models and control over how context is managed. LangSmith Agent Server and Sandboxes allow teams to deploy and run agents securely at scale, whether in the cloud or in their environment. LangSmith Managed Agents and Fleet provide fully managed services to build, deploy, and run mission-critical agents in production.

> **Agent Improvement** helps teams test, monitor, and improve agent performance. LangSmith Observability and Evaluation give teams visibility into how agents behave and measure quality. LangSmith Engine, our in-platform agent, automates improvement by finding errors, writing code and prompt fixes, and monitoring production traffic for regressions. LangSmith Fine-Tuning helps teams turn agent traces into training data to fine-tune and evaluate specialized models.

> **Agent Governance** gives teams centralized control over how agents use models, tools, and data. Our LLM and Tool Gateways help teams manage model budgets and fallbacks, protect sensitive data, and govern tool access and authentication across teams and agents.

## TechCrunch (13 de noviembre de 2026)

> AI agent startup LangChain hits $3B valuation — Agent management startup LangChain has raised a $300 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation led by CapitalG, with participation from investors, including Singapore's GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, and Sapphire Ventures, the company announced on Friday. That's more than double its $1.25 billion valuation when it raised $125 million about a year ago, led by IVP.

> LangChain began in 2022 as a popular open source project that handled the kinds of tasks that early-stage LLMs found difficult, like searching the web, calling APIs, and interacting with databases. As the frontier AI labs delivered harnesses that now competently do those tasks, LangChain turned its focus toward managing AI agents. It has apparently found its stride in this highly competitive area with a simple message: letting businesses own their own intelligence. [...] It also promises that LangSmith will contain agent behavior and is easy to use. [...] LangChain has had a head start and now claims 7,000 LangSmith customers.

## CapitalG — «Doubling Down on LangChain»

> CapitalG is proud to lead LangChain's $300M Series C at a $3B valuation, about a year after we first invested in their Series B. [...] The open source community keeps growing. LangChain began as Harrison's side project in late 2022, and its open source projects have since passed 1 billion downloads.

> A piece we're especially excited about is LangSmith Engine, LangChain's in-platform agent (i.e., an agent that improves other agents), which finds errors in production, writes the code or prompt fix and then watches for regressions. This is the feedback loop that makes an agent better with use.

> Enterprises want a neutral layer they control. The model labs and hyperscalers are all shipping their own agent tooling, and much of it is very good. An enterprise running hundreds of agents across several model providers and clouds still needs one place to build, evaluate, govern and improve all of them. [...] LangSmith is framework-agnostic, works with any model, and runs in LangChain's cloud, self-hosted, or in the customer's own AWS, Azure or Google Cloud account.

## Salesforce Ventures — «Owning Your Intelligence»
