# Databricks Launches New Always-on Genie: Autonomous AI for Complex Enterprise Work

November 11, 2026. SAN FRANCISCO.

## Proactive, Always-on Assistant Brings Enterprise-grade Security and Context

- New second-generation Genie is an always-on enterprise agent that works autonomously, manages long-running work, and proactively brings decisions to people through a new priority inbox and email
- Genie Ontology, the agentic ontology for enterprise AI, automatically maintains understanding of how a company operates across its data, people, and processes to provide horizontal context and deliver accurate, trustworthy answers and actions
- Genie Search expands to 100+ enterprise data sources via Genie and Genie Ontology, including the lakehouse and key business apps like Google Drive, Jira, and Confluence

Databricks announced the second generation of Genie that changes how people get work done: Genie is now an always-on, autonomous agent for enterprise work. Employees can give Genie a goal and trust it to keep work moving. Genie takes ownership of the work, delegates to subagents when needed, tracks progress, follows up on missing inputs, and reaches out when a person needs to make a decision. A new inbox highlights what needs attention, and Genie emails users when they are away.

Work is moving faster, and enterprises now expect AI to take real tasks off their plates. But much of what it takes to do complex projects well lives across multiple platforms and even in people's heads: which accounts need attention, what triggers an escalation, who approves a change. An enterprise assistant can only take that on if it knows the business, can reach the systems teams already use, and follows correct permissions and access controls.

Genie is built for how companies actually work. Every action is grounded in Genie Ontology, an agentic ontology which captures a company's horizontal context, from definitions and metrics to the people and processes behind them, and keeps it current as the business changes. Genie proactively works across all data, structured or unstructured, analytical or operational, inside or outside Databricks to recommend the next best action. Since it runs within a company's existing Databricks environment, Genie is governed by Unity Catalog and Unity Gateway, so every action stays within the controls companies already have in place.

"Genie has made me a 10x more productive CEO, and now it doesn't wait for me to ask," said Ali Ghodsi, Co-founder and CEO of Databricks. "Every day, Genie keeps up with my inbox, preps me for meetings, and runs in the background, coming back only when it needs a decision. It can do that because it knows how our company works: what we mean by revenue, which numbers are official, and who can see what. That's the power of horizontal context, and Genie delivers it."

## Key features

- Sub-agents: Genie delegates tasks to multiple sub-agents to manage work in parallel.
- Priority inbox: Genie now opens to an inbox of items that need attention.
- Email alerts: Each new alert from Genie arrives as its own unread email.
- Governed by default: Every action stays within Unity Catalog permissions and Unity Gateway approval rules and budgets, and is fully auditable.

## Availability

Genie and Genie Ontology are generally available to all customers. Genie Search, Ontology Manager and the new always-on Genie are coming soon to preview and beta programs.
