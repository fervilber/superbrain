## Lunes, 16 de noviembre de 2026 — ARTEX: playbook de caza detallado

> ThreatDefence published a full detection and threat-hunting advisory for **ARTEX**, an open-source agentic penetration framework that researchers and defenders tie to real campaigns (incl. Storm-0940). […] Add the listed **behavioral rules** to your SIEM and cloud-log rules (look for one-second spray rhythms, source-map fetch+immediate internal route calls, and repeated programmatic OAuth flows), block exposed ARTEX consoles, rotate long-lived keys, and test incident playbooks against a non-production ARTEX replica.

## Lunes, 16 de noviembre — Bug de la Responses API de OpenAI (escritura MCP duplicada)

> An OpenAI Developer Community bug report (Nov 16) documents a reproducible behavior where an **approved, native MCP write sometimes executes twice** when the same response continues with a hosted programmatic read. […] Immediately add **idempotency guards** to write endpoints (idempotency keys, transactional checks, or "do-once" markers), run the reproduction in staging, and monitor for duplicated writes in production.

## Domingo, 15 de noviembre — AT&T pone casi 500 flujos agénticos en producción

> AT&T has deployed nearly **500 autonomous AI workflows** across core operations and says its platform now handles **more than 1 million transactions each month**. The company processes about **45 billion AI tokens daily** and plans to triple its production workflow count over the next year. […] AT&T also requires security, privacy, and compliance review for customer-facing workflows, **with human oversight until accuracy reaches the mid-to-high 90% range** within an acceptable risk profile. The reported scale is **company-reported** and should not be treated as independent proof of performance.

## Domingo, 15 de noviembre — digna añade análisis privado de calidad de datos conectado por agentes

> A **Model Context Protocol (MCP) server** connects the platform with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and open-source models. The company says customers can choose which model handles requests, while digna does not access customer data. […] **MCP provides a standard connection layer, but it does not remove the need to control permissions, log model access, and verify answers against source systems.**

## Sábado, 14 de noviembre — LangChain recauda 300 M$ a 3.000 M$ de valoración

> LangChain, an AI-agent development and management company, raised a **$300 million Series C led by CapitalG**, with participation from GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, and Sapphire Ventures. The round values the company at **$3 billion**, more than twice its $1.25 billion valuation from roughly a year earlier. LangChain says its **LangSmith platform has 7,000 customers**, including Harvey, Rippling, Clay, Nvidia, Toyota, and Salesforce.

## Sábado, 14 de noviembre — ElevenLabs previsualiza ElevenAI

> ElevenLabs released **ElevenAI** in preview, combining meeting transcription, searchable meeting context, dictation, live assistance, and agent-driven follow-up actions. […] the value is not merely automatic notes: an agent can turn decisions and conversations into drafts and tasks, but that also raises **consent, accuracy, retention, and authorization issues.**

## Viernes, 13 de noviembre — Google lleva Gemini 4 Hydrogen a Search y a los flujos de desarrollador

> Google released **Gemini 4 Hydrogen**, a faster model designed for multi-step "agentic" work such as research, data analysis, coding, testing, and codebase navigation. Google says Hydrogen completes tasks **2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash**, supports four reasoning levels, and is rolling out globally in Search while also coming to the Gemini API, AI Studio, Gemini apps, and the Antigravity development platform.

## Viernes, 13 de noviembre — United Airlines restringe a los agentes de terceros

> United said it will restrict unauthorized personal AI agents and assistants from accessing its booking and reservation systems. The airline cited **cybersecurity risks from shared credentials, unintended purchases or account changes, and aviation rules that require a human to complete certain check-in acknowledgments.** […] **technical capability does not guarantee permission to automate.**

## Jueves, 12 de noviembre — AWS: Bedrock AgentCore y Strands

> Amazon Bedrock AgentCore added a **stable AgentCore CLI (v1.0)** and an **AgentCore Gateway** that supports model fallback and connecting existing Step Functions workflows as callable tools for agents. The post also announced **Strands Bidi Agents (speech-to-speech) is generally available** and **Strands Box** is in developer preview.
