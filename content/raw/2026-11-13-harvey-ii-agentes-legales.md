# Harvey — The Brief, noviembre de 2026 (publicado el 13 de noviembre de 2026)

Captura por extractos del boletín mensual de producto de Harvey (plataforma de agentes para trabajo legal y profesional). Fecha de captura: 2026-11-13.

> This month's releases let agents take on routine work with the context of your matters and projects, keep large document reviews current as new files arrive, and ground answers in the sources your team already relies on.

> **Harvey II** — Hand off more of your work as agents draw on context from your matters and projects and remember how you prefer to work.

> **Custom Agents and Agent Builder** — Turn your team's processes and standards into reusable Custom Agents, so routine tasks follow your approach every time they run.

> **Space Templates** — Start each new matter or project from your team's best setup by saving a Space, with its phases, tasks, agents, and resources, as a reusable template.

> **Agent Nudges and Routing** — Reference the right resources for better responses with Harvey suggesting relevant vaults, review tables, knowledge sources, connectors, and custom agents for your request.

> **Simplified Governance for Custom Agents and Workflows** — Put new agents and workflows to work as soon as you save them, with admin approval applied only at the sharing levels your organization chooses and updated agent reporting in Command Center.

> **Create Outlook Drafts from Threads, Word, and Custom Agents** — Get work back to clients and colleagues sooner by having Harvey draft emails (with attachments included), directly in your Outlook Drafts folder from a thread, the Word add-in, or a custom agent.

> **Query a Space from a New Thread** — Ask about a matter's materials without opening its Space by attaching a Space and a client matter to any query from the "Work in" selector.
