# Infraestructura y gobernanza de agentes: lanzamientos de la semana 26-oct a 1-nov-2026

> Captura de los resúmenes verbatim publicados en los agregadores agentic.ai/news (semana del 26 de octubre al 1 de noviembre de 2026) y aiagentstore.ai (1 de noviembre de 2026). Se reproducen los párrafos de cada ficha tal como los publica el agregador, que a su vez enlaza a la fuente original.

## BetterCloud lanza gestión del ciclo de vida y control de gasto de agentes de IA (29-oct-2026)

> BetterCloud released AI spend tracking and agent lifecycle management on October 29, 2026, adding the capabilities to its IT governance platform. The launch responds to growing enterprise use of AI and the need to monitor consumption and risk. For those tracking agentic AI products, this points to governance and spending controls becoming part of agent deployment. The announcement highlights real-time token tracking, but the available summary does not specify pricing or the full set of controls.

> Enterprise AI agents now need cost and risk controls too, and BetterCloud's October 29 launch leaves one key governance detail to uncover.

## VoidMob publica un servidor MCP de código abierto para proxies móviles, eSIM y SMS (30-oct-2026)

> VoidMob says its open-source MCP server lets compatible AI agents access mobile proxies, eSIM data plans and SMS through one tool interface. The Tallinn-based connectivity provider announced the server's availability on Oct. 30, 2026. For developers tracking agent infrastructure, this adds mobile connectivity services to the tools agents can access through MCP. The server also includes configurable spending controls, though the announcement does not specify their limits.

## Dataline levanta $10,6 M para suministrar datos a agentes autónomos (1-nov-2026)

> Dataline announced a $10.6 million funding round and a launch cohort for its AI Data Launch partners. The company says its network provides cross-verified data sources, confidence scores, and pay-per-call access for agents; it reports 19.4 million on-chain transactions and a 96.4% agent-task success rate to date.

> **Why it matters:** Agent reliability depends on more than the model's reasoning. For operators building agents that make decisions or trigger transactions, verified, current, machine-readable data and a way to measure confidence can be as important as the choice of model.

> **Try/watch:** Before connecting an agent to paid or irreversible actions, record the source, freshness, confidence, and fallback behavior for every external data call.

## Coforge presenta una "fábrica de software autónoma" (29-oct-2026)

> Coforge has introduced its Autonomous Software Factory, coordinating AI agents across coding, testing, validation, code review and quality assurance. […] Coforge says specialized agents can work in parallel inside secure execution environments, though details on the system's results are not included in the summary.

## Otras piezas de la semana (para contexto)

- **Base de datos autónoma A2A de Oracle** (blog de Oracle, Mark Hornick): un A2A Server multitenant gestionado, embebido en Autonomous AI Database Serverless y Dedicated Region (19c y 26ai), que expone agentes construidos con el framework Oracle Select AI Agent a clientes A2A (Gemini Enterprise, Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio) sin desplegar infraestructura de servidor aparte; controles de identidad y autorización nativos de la base de datos (OCI IAM, OIDC/OAuth 2.1, listas de acceso, VPD, rate limits). Complementa a un MCP Server de la misma base de datos: A2A expone _agentes_, MCP expone _herramientas_.
- **Fundación Linux / Agentic AI Foundation**: el 22 de octubre de 2026 anunció cuatro proyectos nuevos, entre ellos _Agent Skills_ y _Open Responses_, para portabilidad de capacidades e interoperabilidad orientada a modelos.
- **IBM**: el 2 de octubre detalló el descubrimiento multiplataforma de agentes construidos sobre Microsoft Foundry y Google Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform a través de watsonx Orchestrate.
- **Encuesta de LF Research (20-oct-2026)**: la IA generativa reduce a la mitad el tiempo de codificación en abierto, pero el 25% de los _pull requests_ entrantes se consideran "AI slop"; el 59% de los mantenedores reporta crecimiento del volumen de contribuciones.
