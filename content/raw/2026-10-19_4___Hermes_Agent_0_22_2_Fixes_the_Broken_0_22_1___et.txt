Hermes Agent 0.22.2 Fixes the Broken 0.22.1
By st0rmshadow
Oct 18, 2026
Leave a comment

Nous Research pushed Hermes Agent 0.22.2 on October 15 (still the latest release), and the notes are refreshingly honest: 0.22.1 was built from the wrong commit. It shipped unreleased gateway work and could leave the Microsoft Store app stuck on “Connecting.” 0.22.2 is what 0.22.1 was meant to be.

The gateway starts properly after a reboot. It used to mistake a reused PID for a running gateway and refuse to start under systemd, launchd or Docker.
Long local-model turns survive better on laptops, with streaming progress and sleep-aware stall timers.
Known issue: sessions run under the 0.22.1 gateway can’t be deleted in 0.22.2.

The local-model fixes are the ones I care about. If you run Hermes against a local server, update.

Happy Hacking!
