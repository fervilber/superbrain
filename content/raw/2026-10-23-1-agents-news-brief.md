## Source 2: Comprehensive Review — Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems

URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-026-11731-2
Published: October 16, 2026 | Artificial Intelligence Review, Springer Nature
Open Access

### Abstract

Autonomous agents and multi-agent systems have rapidly advanced with the integration of large language models (LLMs), enabling increasingly capable reasoning, planning, tool use, and collaboration. However, the field remains fragmented by inconsistent architectural terminology, limited cross-domain comparison, and heterogeneous evaluation practices.

This review addresses these gaps through a two-stage PRISMA-informed search combining Scopus with structured manual screening, covering 107 peer-reviewed original studies published between 2018 and 2026.

### Key Contributions

- **Unified five-module architecture**: profile, memory, planning, action, and security
- **Cross-domain comparison**: agent capabilities across ten application domains
- **Six evaluation dimensions**: task performance, efficiency, quality, robustness, safety, and interpretability

### Key Findings

- Explicit integration of domain knowledge consistently improves performance
- Planner–executor–reflector role specialization appears across diverse applications
- Technical performance often exceeds validated real-world utility
- Robustness, safety, and interpretability remain particularly under-evaluated
