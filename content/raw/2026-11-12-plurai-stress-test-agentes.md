## Extractos verbatim

> **What's the deal?** Plurai has raised $13 million in seed funding to expand a platform that simulates corporate environments and stress-tests autonomous AI agents before they go live. Team8 led the round, with participation from U&I, Velocity, Mercer, and angel investors including CyberArk founder Udi Mokady and Google DeepMind researcher Reah Miyara.

> **What's the endgame?** Founded by former Nexar researchers Ilan Kadar and Elad Levi, the New York-based startup borrows an approach from autonomous driving: simulation. It creates a digital twin of an organisation's tools, knowledge bases, and policies, then runs thousands of synthetic interactions—including adversarial ones—[...] models from OpenAI and Anthropic on accuracy at roughly 40 to 44 times lower cost.

> **Why now?** Building an agent is easy; making it reliable across unpredictable interactions is not. Plurai also opened general availability of its platform, previously limited to select partners, adding a free tier for developers. It employs about 12 people across Israel and the US and plans to double headcount by the end of 2026.

> **By the numbers:** The round sits in the top 5% of seed rounds in the US over the trailing 48 months, a sample of 17,141 deals. Plurai's own claims are bolder still: design partners reportedly cut failures reaching users by 82% and operating costs by as much as 97%—figures drawn from early customer work that await independent validation.
