# Captura: RingCX pone agentes de IA y humanos en el mismo panel de supervisión

> Texto completo del artículo del 28 de octubre de 2026. Incluye cifras del informe de resultados del tercer trimestre de 2026 (22 de octubre) y escenarios de uso publicados por el fabricante.

## RingCentral Puts AI and Human Agents on 1 Contact Center Dashboard

RingCX now puts AI agents in the same live supervisor view, quality rubric and staffing forecast as human agents.

### The Gist

- **One dashboard for AI and human agents.** RingCX now extends live supervision, quality scoring, journey analytics and staffing forecasts to AI agents, including RingCentral AI Receptionist (AIR) and AIR Pro.
- **One quality rubric.** Supervisors can score AI agent conversations against the same rubric they use for human agents and step into live AI conversations.
- **Adoption is ahead of coverage.** AIR customers nearly quadrupled year over year, but CEO Vlad Shmunis said last week that AI still answers a very small share of calls on RingCentral's network.

RingCentral wants contact center supervisors to watch, grade and schedule AI agents with the same tools they use for human agents.

The company announced new AI capabilities for RingCX on Oct. 28 that put AI and human agents in a single supervisor view. RingCX, RingCentral's own contact center product, now extends live supervision, quality scoring, journey analytics and staffing forecasts to AI agents, including AIR and AIR Pro. RingCentral is showing the features at ICMI 2026 in Orlando this week.

The launch follows RingCentral's third-quarter earnings report on Oct. 22. On that call, executives said customers are buying AI products quickly, while AI still handles a small fraction of the calls on RingCentral's network.

### What RingCX Now Does With AI Agents

According to RingCentral, the new capabilities let contact centers:

- Transfer conversations between AI and human agents in both directions while preserving context.
- Show AI agent interactions in the same live view as human agents, so supervisors can intervene in real time.
- Score every AI agent interaction, with sentiment, against the rubric used for human agents.
- Track the full customer journey across AI and human agents.
- Forecast staffing across AI and human agent capacity together.

The announcement names AIR and AIR Pro as the AI agents covered.

"With RingCX, leaders see every customer conversation in one place, whether an AI agent or a human agent is handling it. They can step in sooner, hold quality to one standard, and plan staffing for the whole workforce, so more customers get their issue resolved in one go, with no need to call back," said Jim Dvorkin, SVP of customer experience products at RingCentral.

### How RingCentral Says the Features Work in Practice

_Editor's note: These scenarios are illustrative examples from RingCentral's announcement, not named customer deployments._

| Scenario                     | How RingCX Handles It                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Health plan claim denial** | An AI agent answers a member's initial questions and gathers details while a supervisor watches live. If the member needs empathy, the supervisor moves the call to a human agent with **context intact**, so the member doesn't repeat the story. |
| **Retail holiday staffing**  | A workforce planner schedules AI agents into the seasonal forecast. AI agents take routine shipping and return questions **overnight and on weekends**, and human agents take escalations, covering the surge without temporary hires.             |
| **Bank fraud disputes**      | A quality leader sees AI-handled fraud dispute calls have the **highest escalation rate** of any intent. The team routes those calls straight to human agents, so fraud customers reach a person on the first attempt.                             |

### Why Supervisors Need to See the Handoff

EVP Eyecare is one RingCentral customer planning to use the new features to catch gaps between its AI agent and its staff.

"We've been finding that when a customer's request doesn't match what our AI agent expects, that gap can slip through until the patient calls back frustrated. With these new RingCX features, we'll now have one system that can catch that kind of mismatch before it becomes a bad experience, instead of after," said Tasha McIntosh, senior patient care manager at EVP Eyecare.

Sheila McGee-Smith, president and principal analyst at McGee-Smith Analytics, said shared visibility could make companies more willing to deploy AI agents. She also flagged a problem coming from the other side of the call.

"Bringing AI and human agents under common visibility will give businesses more confidence to begin or expand agentic AI," McGee-Smith said. "The next challenge will be personal AI agents: as consumers increasingly use agents to act on their behalf, contact centers will need to recognize, route, and measure both inbound and outbound AI-agent traffic. Understanding the full AI-to-human customer journey will become essential."

### AI Onboarding, Mobile Agents and Microsoft Teams

RingCentral also introduced AI Onboarding for RingCX. Administrators describe the configuration they want in natural language. RingCentral AI builds a configuration plan, executes it through RingCX APIs and runs a simulated routing test before go-live.

RingCX is now available on mobile, so agents can take routed RingCX voice calls through the RingCentral mobile app. It is also available inside Microsoft Teams.

### The Earnings Backdrop: Fast AI Growth, Small Share of Calls

RingCentral reported third-quarter revenue of $676 million, up 5.9% year over year, and raised its full-year outlook. On the earnings call, executives spent most of their time on AI product adoption.

| Product                              | What It Does                                                                                            | Q3 2026 Adoption                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **AIR (AI Receptionist)**            | Answers inbound calls, handles routine inquiries and routes callers, starting with missed calls.        | **22,000+ customers**, up nearly 4x year over year                                                                 |
| **ACE (AI Conversation Expert)**     | Analyzes conversations to surface call drivers and support scorecard grading and coaching.              | **Approaching 7,000 customers**, up 60% year over year                                                             |
| **CEB (Customer Engagement Bundle)** | A lightweight contact center for businesses that handle customer calls without a formal contact center. | **14,000+ paying customers**, up nearly 50% quarter over quarter; nearly two-thirds bought at least one AI product |
| **AIR Pro**                          | Voice-first AI agents that complete multistep workflows and hand off to human agents with context.      | Not disclosed; RingCentral cited "strong adoption" in the quarter                                                  |

Shmunis said AI-handled calls are still a small slice of the traffic RingCentral carries.

"I can tell you that our traffic growth has been strong but linear. Our AI growth has been explosive, obviously. But overall penetration of AI and AI-answered calls on our network is still very small percentage-wise," Shmunis said.

RingCentral also said customers using at least one paid AI product now account for about 15% of total annual recurring revenue (ARR), or more than $400 million, doubling year over year. That figure counts the full spend of any customer who buys an AI product, so it is larger than revenue from AI products alone. CFO Vaibhav Agarwal said those customers have higher average revenue per user and net retention above 100%.

AIR is priced by usage, so customers pay more as it handles more calls. Agarwal said AIR sells mostly to small businesses today and is moving into mid-market and enterprise accounts.

### From Missed Calls to Multistep Workflows

President and COO Kira Makagon said a national fitness operator started with ACE to identify call drivers, then added AIR to handle a high volume of recurring inquiries. The customer has expanded its investment with RingCentral 4x, she said.

Destination Pet, a nationwide pet care provider, started with ACE to analyze call transcripts and expanded to AIR Pro to automate appointment scheduling, confirmations and record updates across locations.

Makagon also described a technology and procurement provider for senior living that added AIR Pro to verify callers, match them to their records, identify why they're calling and act on the request, handing off to a human when needed.

"In just the first months, this customer achieved an over 95% accuracy rate in end-to-end workflow completion," Makagon said. RingCentral did not name the customer.

Shmunis said healthcare and financial services remain RingCentral's strongest verticals for AI. He also said AI adoption through its carrier and telecom partners moves slower than direct sales.

RingCentral is also bringing its conversation data into outside AI tools. Makagon said RingCentral introduced plugins for OpenAI's ChatGPT and MCP connectors for Anthropic's Claude this quarter, and was selected as a Claude for Small Business launch partner. Thousands of customers were already active on them in less than a month, she said.

### Where RingCX Fits

RingCX sits next to RingCentral Contact Center, which RingCentral sells through its partnership with NiCE. Asked about the contact center agreement on the call, Shmunis said, "So we still have very much multiple years left on this contract." He positioned RingCX for the mid-market today and said he expects it to become attractive to larger enterprises.

Shmunis also split the market by company size when describing where AI will take over and where humans stay.

"While smaller businesses very much pride themselves and differentiate on their ability to answer customer inquiries with a human being, and that is at least for the time being, basically, regardless of how good AI gets, because that is part of the service that they provide. Larger enterprises, it is a call center, and the AI can do a very good job there," Shmunis said.

### Notas del capturador

- El movimiento es de **gestión de fuerza de trabajo mixta**: los agentes de IA entran en la previsión de personal, la rúbrica de calidad y el análisis de recorrido del cliente, no solo en el enrutado.
- El handoff bidireccional con contexto preservado es la pieza técnica que permite la supervisión en vivo; el agente deja de ser una caja negra separada del supervisor.
- La analista Sheila McGee-Smith introduce el mismo problema que Zocdoc ataca desde el otro lado: **agentes personales actuando en nombre del consumidor** obligarán a reconocer, enrutar y medir tráfico entrante y saliente de agentes.
- Contraste de escala relevante: adopción de producto de IA muy alta (22.000+ clientes de AIR, 15% del ARR) frente a penetración real de llamadas atendidas por IA «muy pequeña en porcentaje».
