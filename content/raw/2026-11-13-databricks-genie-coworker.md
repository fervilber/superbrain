# Databricks — El nuevo Genie (12 de noviembre de 2026)

Captura por extractos del blog oficial de Databricks y del resumen diario de AI Agents Directory. Fecha de captura: 2026-11-13.

## Blog de Databricks

> **Genie is now an autonomous AI coworker that handles long-running tasks on your behalf, operating within the guardrails you set.**
> • Genie Ontology, an agentic ontology built by agents and governed by people, provides trusted business context, while Genie Search finds information across your lakehouse and business systems to help Genie deliver accurate and complete answers.
> • Unity Gateway lets admins control tool access, trace actions, and set spending limits to keep autonomy under control.

> AI assistants can run analyses and summarize meetings, but employees still manage the work around them. That's because most enterprise work is continuous, complex, and data-intensive. To monitor pipeline performance or manage budgets, someone has to track changes, decide what matters, and take action.

> Today, we're introducing the new **Genie**: an autonomous AI coworker that proactively handles long-running enterprise work for you. Genie stays on the job continuously, tracking progress, following up on missing inputs, and taking action as new information arrives. You set the guardrails, supervise its work, and approve where needed, and Genie takes care of the rest.

> You can work with Genie through Genie One, email, Slack, and Microsoft Teams. Give it a job and access to the tools it needs, and it takes responsibility for ongoing work. It checks in when it needs input or approval, and it adapts to your feedback.

> For example, Genie can monitor incoming insurance claims for emerging fraud patterns and loss-ratio drift, then assemble the case file and route it to underwriters when a segment moves outside guidelines. In marketing, Genie can manage campaigns around the clock, investigate any underperformance, and propose weekly budget changes for approval.

> What makes this possible is context from Genie Ontology, which tells Genie how your business works—from what counts as an active customer to which revenue metric is authoritative. Personal memory retains your preferences and instructions, so you don't have to repeat them for every job.

> Thousands of customers use Genie in production, including Zillow, Unilever, adidas, Atlassian, and AstraZeneca.
