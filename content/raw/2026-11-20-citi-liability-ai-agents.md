# Citi Makes Customers Responsible for Third-Party AI Agent Activity

Unite.AI / TechCrunch — 18 de noviembre de 2026

The new **Section H** applies when third-party AI agents or agentic AI access accounts or digital services through Citi Online or Citi Mobile. Citi defines an AI agent broadly as **software or other technology that takes autonomous, semi-autonomous, or non-autonomous action** for a customer or someone acting on the customer's behalf. Agents, tools, functions, and features provided by Citi itself through those channels are **excluded**.

## La actividad del agente pasa a ser autorizada por el cliente

Citi's November 2026 update to its Citi Online User Agreement makes customers responsible for actions by third-party AI agents they allow into their accounts, **including activity using credentials later shared or leaked**. The customer is responsible for actions or errors resulting from:

- an agent's incorrect interpretation or execution of instructions,
- unintended actions, and
- actions exceeding the authority granted,

except as otherwise permitted under applicable law. The agreement also makes **monitoring the agent's activities for accuracy, completeness, and compliance the customer's responsibility rather than Citi's**.

If you give an agent your login and those credentials are "shared, leaked, or otherwise distributed", Citi "will consider any access and actions taken with the Online Credentials as authorized by You". Citi has **no obligation to investigate those actions** "even if" they "conflict with any prompts or other instructions" you gave the agent.

## Cascada de la industria

- **JPMorgan y Bank of America** movieron primero (octubre 2026).
- **PNC** cambió sus términos el **18 de noviembre**.
- **Citizens** envió correos a clientes.
- **Wells Fargo**: acuerdo efectivo el **24 de noviembre de 2026**; dice que "no monitorea, examina ni revisa las actividades de agentes de IA" y que para revocar un agente el cliente "debe conservar evidencia de dicha revocación". Clasifica las transacciones iniciadas por agentes como autorizadas por el cliente.
- **Schwab** bloquea agentes no autorizados y ha prometido sus propios servidores MCP.

## Lectura regulatoria y matices

- La **Reg E** sigue protegiendo al cliente frente a transacciones _no autorizadas_; el debate es si dar voluntariamente acceso a un agente convierte lo que haga en "autorizado". Postura dominante: "**the bot is you**" — si el bot lo rompe, el cliente lo asume.
- **El banco central de Singapur (MAS)** propone en su lugar un **"know your agent protocol"** (protocolo de conocimiento del agente), y la primera oleada de canales de agente sancionados por el banco está llegando.
